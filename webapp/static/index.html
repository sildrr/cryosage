ACQUISITION PARAMETERS

300 kV and 2.7 mm are prefilled; verify your microscope settings. Movies may require gain information; gain configuration uploads are not supported. Dose is required for micrographs and movies.

RECONSTRUCTION GOAL

Initial sizing: crYOLO image-only measurement when available, otherwise an explicitly recorded visual estimate. Objective pilot gate: >2,000 core particles (CryoSift <3), with verified supplemental views <4 and three supported shape groups (NCC 0.90). A failed pilot is reported, not silently promoted.
